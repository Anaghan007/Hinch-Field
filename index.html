Frequently Asked Questions

Where to buy premium oversized graphic t-shirts online?+

HinchField offers a curated collection of heavyweight oversized graphic T-Shirt with streetwear and retro aesthetics. COD available across India.

How do I order custom name or photo t-shirts?+

Select any T-Shirt from our Custom Category, add your text or upload instructions in the notes, and checkout. We confirm the design on WhatsApp before printing.

Do you offer minimalist text t-shirts?+

Yes, our Minimalist collection features clean typography, micro-dots, and subtle chest hits.

Are cropped boxy t-shirts available for women?+

Absolutely. Our Cropped Boxy collection is tailored specifically for women with a relaxed silhouette.

Do you sell premium polo t-shirts?+

Yes, we offer premium polo T-Shirt with structured collars and breathable fabrics.

Where to buy anime and gaming T-Shirt in India?+

Our Anime & Gaming category features officially inspired streetwear graphics.

What is the delivery time and tracking process?+

Orders are processed within 24 hours and delivered within 48 hours. Track live via My Orders.

What are your shipping and delivery charges?+

Below ₹999: ₹99. ₹999-₹1499: ₹49. Above ₹1499: FREE across India.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?+

Yes, COD is available across India. Pay at your doorstep after verifying the 4-digit OTP.

Is there a return or exchange policy?+

Strict No Return / No Exchange policy due to customisation options.

Do you ship worldwide?+

Yes, worldwide shipping. International orders confirmed via WhatsApp with live currency.

How do I track my HinchField order?+

Use "My Orders" or "Track" from the menu to see real-time stages.
